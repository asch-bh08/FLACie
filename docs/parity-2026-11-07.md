# Phone ↔ web parity check, 7 Nov 2026

The owner asked for everything on the phone to be on the web, and the other way round. This compares the phone's screens and Settings with the web's pages and Settings. The check is by code and labels; walking through every screen by hand is still to do.

## Fixed tonight (web)

- **Song and album preview before downloading.** Search › More music: a tap on an album card or a song opens it full screen first. Albums show the cover, facts, Download album, and their songs (each downloadable). Songs show Download, Find on YouTube, and Other versions. Nothing is fetched until Download. Test: 1i-download-tags "More music: … open full screen first".
- **Volume limit in Settings › Playback.** Off, 85%, 70% or 50%, kept in this browser. Test: 1z-settings-sound.
- **Artist search.** Albums and Playlists sit side by side, four whole cards per half.

## Already on both sides (checked)

Even out volume; fade between songs; equalizer (web: Now Playing › Sound); sleep timer; lyrics; DJ settings; Autoplay; Fetch ahead; daily charts; import a playlist; Jam; Devices / Connect; headphone test; Retro; share links; recommended songs on playlists; other versions; the Hi-Res download.

## Phone only, and right to stay so

Offline music; keep free space; swipe actions; face buttons; Android Auto; lyrics in the car; notifications; music on this phone, NAS and Plex sources; iPod sync. These are things a browser can't do, or that belong to the device.

## Web only, still to look at

- **Alexa setup.** The web has it in Account; the phone has no link to it. Suggestion: a Settings row on the phone that opens the web page.
- **Artist names fixes and the rest of Server admin.** Web only on purpose: the phone's "Server admin" opens the web dashboard.
- **Charts page** (/charts). The phone has the chart settings and the charts in Explore, but no list page of its own. Needs a closer look.
- **Phone, not yet released.** The Jellyfin copy of a playlist keeps repeated songs (AccountSync `.distinct()` removed, commit after b121). It goes out with the next beta.
