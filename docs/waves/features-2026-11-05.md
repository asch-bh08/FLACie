# Feature list, 5 Nov 2026 (approved by the owner; specs and wave plan next)

Covers, speed, search (first):
- F1 Never a blank cover: Jellyfin > song's own release > album > folder picture > artist photo > generated cover. Untagged songs (137, e.g. Eminem/Singles/Eminem_Lose_Yourself.mp3.mp3) get the artist from file/folder names. One background pass on frank fetches every cover and tile.
- F2 One cover source: the phone takes every cover from frank (today it asks Deezer itself and keeps its own).
- F3 Library copy on the phone: library list and cover tiles kept on the phone (tens of MB), pages open instantly, update in the background. NO song cache (Offline downloads is for that).
- F4 Next song loaded ahead while the current plays, deleted once played.
- F5 Weak signal: small Opus (~128k) stream, back to FLAC when good; no stalls.
- F6 Search like Spotify: top result = the artist you play / have most (The Prodigy, not Prodigy of Mobb Deep); their top songs beside it; Popular, Albums, Singles & EPs, Playlists (Best of, Radio, decades, genres), similar names. Phone and web.
- F7 Remixes and SoundCloud via yt-dlp (no API key): remix-aware YouTube search (keep "(X Remix)", check uploader and length). SoundCloud joins the source race. Rank: lossless > SoundCloud original download (producer's free download, often WAV/FLAC) > YouTube ~160k Opus > SoundCloud 128k. Quickest plays first; anything lossy is queued for the lossless upgrade.
- F8 Re-check every b105 fix as if broken (web, emulator, owner's phone if on adb). Ships with commit "Lyrics glide in their own panel; an artist search finds their radio..." (done, not released).
