# F19 Mini player

## Summary

The web's picture-in-picture mini player (flacie.js, documentPictureInPicture) is plain. Make it match the full player: colours from the cover,
the loudness-shaped seek bar with times, shuffle, repeat, heart, DJ and mic buttons, a "Next:" line, the current synced lyric line, and a layout
that turns into one bar (cover left) when the window is wide and short. The browser's own frame (address bar, close button) cannot change.

## In scope

- Everything inside the mini player window

## Out of scope

- The browser's window frame

## Acceptance criteria

```yaml criteria
AC1: {text: "The mini player shows the seek bar with times, shuffle, repeat, heart, DJ and mic, and a Next line", verify: "test:ipodsync/tests/web-smoke/mini-player.spec.ts"}
AC2: {text: "A window wider than it is tall shows one bar with the cover on the left", verify: "test:ipodsync/tests/web-smoke/mini-player.spec.ts"}
```
