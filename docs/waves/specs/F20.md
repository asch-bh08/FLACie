# F20 Car-friendly Android Auto

## Summary

Android Auto draws media apps itself; an app only chooses which custom buttons exist, their order, icons and labels, the browse tree, and the
song's text. Within that: put the mic (Ask the DJ) in the first custom-action slot so it sits on the right next to the playback controls (check
on the owner's car which slot that is), keep skip buttons in fixed places (slot reservation), at most four custom buttons with large, simple
icons (mic, heart, shuffle toggle, DJ on/off), a browse tree that is two taps deep with cover grids for playlists and albums, the DJ's caption
as the song's subtitle while it talks, and "Hey Google, play X on FLACie" going to the DJ's understanding.

## In scope

- Custom actions, their order and icons; slot reservation; browse tree layout hints; play-from-search to the DJ

## Out of scope

- Drawing our own car screens (Android Auto does not allow it for media apps)
