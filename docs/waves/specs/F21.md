# F21 Frequently played

## Summary

A "Frequently played" row on Home (web and phone): songs the person really listens to, ranked by real plays. A real play is one heard to at
least 60% (or 4 minutes); a song skipped early does not count, and a skip within 30 s counts against it. A song needs 3 real plays to show,
recent plays weigh more (half-life 30 days). "Listen again" keeps every played song as today, but moves further down Home, below Frequently played.

## In scope

- Real-play counting from the listening log (server) and the phone's own history
- The Home row on web and phone; Listen again unchanged but moved further down Home

## Out of scope

- Changing what the listening log records
