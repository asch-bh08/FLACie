# F22 DJ knows your favourites

## Summary

"Play my favourite Prodigy songs" plays the person's real favourites by that artist without them having hearted anything: ranked by real
plays (F21), hearted songs and repeats first, early skips last; then their most popular songs to fill to at least 15. Works for an artist, an
album, a genre or a decade ("my favourite 90s songs"), and "my favourites" alone.

## In scope

- The DJ understanding and the ranking

## Out of scope

- Changing the Favourites list itself
