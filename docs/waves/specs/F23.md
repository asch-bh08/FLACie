# F23 Spotify's playlists, downloaded as you go

## Summary

"Playlists for you" shows Spotify's real playlists (This Is <artist>, All Out 90s, Rock Classics, Today's Top Hits, genres), read with no key
from Spotify's public embed pages (open.spotify.com/embed/playlist/<id>, the __NEXT_DATA__ JSON: name, track titles and artists), refreshed
about daily on frank. Only the track LISTS are kept (a few KB each); songs are matched to the library like Import. Songs not in the library are
downloaded only as interest shows, never all at once (every playlist in full would be terabytes):
- Opening or playing a playlist (or a song from it): download all its missing songs, in play order, the next few first.
- Searching an artist: download that artist's This Is playlist's missing songs, top half first, the rest after.
- Popular lists (charts, and decades and genres the person listens to): only the first 2 missing songs each, so they start instantly.
- A daily cap on background downloads (default 150 songs) and a storage floor (stop when the music disk has under 50 GB free).
Decades, genres and charts come from a fixed list of Spotify playlist IDs; an artist's This Is playlist is found through Spotify's own search
when possible, else Deezer's equivalent ("100% <artist>"), else FLACie's own Best of. Our own lists stay as the fallback, fixed to one version
per song (no instrumentals; album version preferred) and sized to the artist (half their distinct songs, 10 to 50), hits first.

## In scope

- Embed reader, playlist catalog, matching, the download policy, the fallback fixes

## Out of scope

- Spotify accounts or the official Web API
