# F24 Same-name artists sorted song by song

## Summary

Some of The Prodigy's songs are tagged "Prodigy", which is also a different artist (Prodigy of Mobb Deep: H.N.I.C., Return of the Mac). So
The Prodigy's search page, Popular, Radio and Best of miss those songs, and "Prodigy" mixes both. Decide per song, never by merging names:
a song tagged "X" goes to "The X" when its album or title is also The X's in the library (same album name, or same song title), or when the
catalog (Deezer search of title + "The X", cached) says it is The X's. Everything else stays with "X". "X of Y" / "X feat. Z" spellings of
the same person ("Prodigy of Mobb Deep") join "X" when that "X" is not The X. The file's tags are not changed, but everywhere FLACie shows them (search, artist pages, song rows, Now Playing, the DJ) a re-sorted song shows and counts as "The X", so searching "prodigy" gives ONE The Prodigy with all of its songs.

## In scope

- Server grouping (search, artists, made lists, DJ), and the phone's twin

## Out of scope

- Rewriting tags in files
