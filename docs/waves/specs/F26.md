# F26 Round 4 (owner, 5 Nov evening, with screenshots of Spotify's search)

Local already did, on ui/living-sleeve (read before starting): playlists right under the Top result, up to 8 (WebPlaylistPick.ArtistRow), a list
that holds the artist's songs counts for an artist search (WebPlaylistPick.Holds) but downloads nothing until opened, Spotify's fixed lists read
once a day (SpotifyPlaylists.WarmFixed), top songs lead with songs on several albums with at most 2 per album (ArtistPage.Of), the Playlists chip
in the All/Songs/Albums/Artists row (ResultSections ExtraKinds/UnderKinds), a plain Filters button, a solid band behind the floating search box.
Background playlist downloads are kind "Playlist" (kept out of Search's Downloads panel). The web mic records in Brave (/api/dj/hear, Whisper).

## F26.1 Many real playlists, ours last
"This is how many playlists we should have, and the made-for-you ones should be a last resort because they're shit." Spotify shows ~15 for
"the prodigy": Best Of by fans, "The Prodigy Mix", "Oldschool Anthems of Rave 88-94", "The Prodigy - Remixes", "the prodigy all songs"...
- Deezer's playlist search (api.deezer.com/search/playlist?q=<artist>, public, no key) gives exactly these: fans' and editors' lists. Show the
  relevant ones: title holds the artist (exact words, The kept: SpotifyPlaylists.IsEditorsBestOf's rule) or the list holds several of the
  artist's songs; at least 8 tracks; rank editors first, then by how many of the artist's songs and nb_tracks/fans; drop near-duplicate titles.
- Plus SoundCloud artist sets (F25.3) and Spotify's fixed lists holding the artist (Holds).
- Our own made lists only when fewer than 4 real lists were found, and always after them.
- Artist search row: up to 15 (2 rows of cards on desktop, scroll on phone). Nothing downloads for lists shown only (F23 policy unchanged:
  opened/played = all, artist best-of = top half; "popular 2 songs" only for lists the search names).

## F26.2 "Featuring <artist>" row
Spotify's artist page has "Featuring The Prodigy" (editorial lists that contain them: Born in the 80s, Big Beat, 303 Classics...). "This is good
but don't just copy it." Ours: a row on the artist search and the artist page, "Lists with <artist> in", from the lists already read (Spotify
fixed lists, Deezer editorial lists found while searching, SoundCloud sets), each card showing which of their songs it holds ("Firestarter,
Breathe + 2"). Not a copy of Spotify's layout or wording.

## F26.3 Filters that filter
"Make the playlist filter only show playlists and albums only show albums; the separate filter is where you customise more with genre."
- The kind chips (All, Songs, Albums, Artists, Playlists) are one choice: picking Playlists shows only playlists (every list that matches, the
  F23 filter list), Albums only albums, etc. Today Playlists turns on next to another chip (screenshot: Albums and Playlists both lit).
- Genre (and decade) live in the Filters button's panel and narrow whatever kind is picked (genre chips under the Playlists chip go).
- The genre list is cleaned: no "Genre" or "Other" chips.

## F26.4 The DJ's favourites are the real ones
"Play my favourite prodigy songs" gave songs played once or never. Causes (Local looked, DjFavourites.Rank):
- only FLACie's listen log counts; Jellyfin's own play counts (UserSession.PlayStats, all apps, before the log began) are ignored. Add
  IDjAskUser.JellyfinPlays(Track) (DjAskHost: s.PlayStats by JellyfinId) and count max(log real plays, Jellyfin plays).
- the fill to 15 sorts by Popularity, which is 0 until looked up, then by title: the alphabet. Fill with hits: Popularity, then songs on several
  of their albums (same rule as ArtistPage.Of), then year; LookUpPopularityAsync first for the scope (a handful of Deezer asks, cached).
- skipped songs are appended at the end: leave them out.
- the DJ says what it did: "You've played 6 of their songs a lot; then their biggest hits."

## F26.5 The DJ shows what it is doing; captions move
"Add like what it's doing / thinking and change the subtitle location to where the current downloads are, in a little own bar above the now
playing bar."
- A small bar just above the player bar (where the "Getting 6 songs" chip sits; that chip joins it) shows the DJ's state: Listening… (with a
  level), Heard: "play my favourite prodigy songs", Thinking…, Finding your favourites…, Downloading 3 songs…, then the caption of what it says,
  in time with the voice (F17's timing). It hides when the DJ is idle.
- Captions leave their current place for this bar. Phone and car are not part of this (Local does them).

## F26.6 Imports show every song
Importing "80s - 90s rally" (99 songs): the playlist shows only the 24 downloaded. "We should be able to see the whole playlist when imported
but not downloaded: grey them out or tag them downloading, and a way to see the actual downloads of each song for that playlist."
- The playlist page lists every song of the import in its order: owned ones normal; missing ones greyed with their state (Waiting, Searching,
  Downloading 40 %, Couldn't find it: Retry), live.
- A "Downloads for this playlist" view (from the playlist page and from the Import row's Open) lists that import's jobs only: source found,
  quality, progress, failures with the reason.
- Greyed songs can't be played; they become normal rows as they land, in place.
