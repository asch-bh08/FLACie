# F4 Next song loaded ahead

## Summary

While a song plays, the next one is loaded into a temporary buffer (freed once played or skipped past), so skipping and the change between songs are instant. Phone and web.

## In scope

- Phone: next song buffered; web: next song preloaded

## Out of scope

- Keeping songs after they play
