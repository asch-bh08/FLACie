# F5 Lower quality on weak signal

## Summary

On a weak or slow connection the phone plays a small Opus stream (~128 kbps, Jellyfin transcode) and switches back to FLAC when the connection is good, so songs never stall. Downloaded songs are always full quality.

## In scope

- Measuring throughput, choosing the stream per song

## Out of scope

- Switching mid-song
