# F6 Search like Spotify

## Summary

Search shows the artist you mean (the one you play and have most: The Prodigy, not Prodigy of Mobb Deep) as the top result with their top songs beside it, then Popular, Albums, Singles & EPs, Playlists (Best of, Radio, decades, genres) and similar names, with no big empty areas. Same on phone and web.
